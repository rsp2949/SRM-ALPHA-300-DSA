

# Day 24 – Palindrome Depth

## Overview

Practised palindrome problems using expand-around-center and two-pointer techniques to identify, count, and construct palindromic strings.

## Problems Completed

| No. | Problem | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Longest Palindromic Substring | Expand Around Center |
| 2 | Palindromic Substrings | Expand Around Center |
| 3 | Shortest Palindrome | Two Pointers, Prefix Checking |

## Concepts Practised

- Expanding around odd and even centers.
- Finding the longest palindromic substring.
- Counting palindromic substrings.
- Checking palindromic prefixes.
- Constructing the shortest palindrome by adding characters.

Improved understanding of palindrome detection, center expansion, and two-pointer string processing.
